# OnMotor Hero Video

`onmotor-riders-hero-ai.webm` is the original 13-second, silent 1920×1080 Hero video for the shop home page.

It was created on 2026-08-30 from three original AI-generated scenes—road riding, adventure/off-road riding, and urban riding—and composed locally into a loop-ready WebM. `onmotor-riders-hero-poster.png` is its static fallback for visitors who prefer reduced motion or whose browser cannot play the video.

No third-party stock footage, product image, logo, or brand mark is used in these assets.
